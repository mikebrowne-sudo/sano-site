## 3. Electronic signature — validity & evidence
Signing is by **typed name** with an on-screen attestation, plus a recorded
timestamp and an audit-log entry. Clause 18.3 provides for it:

> *"This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts… By typing
> their name to sign, the Contractor confirms they have read, understood and agree
> to this Agreement."*

**Please confirm:** the typed-name e-signature + attestation + timestamp is
sufficient and legally binding under the Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017 for
these agreements, and whether we should capture anything further (e.g. IP / device)
for stronger non-repudiation.

## 4. Contractor insurance minimum wording
Clause 9.1:

> *"…public liability insurance appropriate to the Services — the Principal's
> minimum requirement is $1,000,000 for residential work and $2,000,000 for
> commercial work — and must provide evidence of cover on request and before
> commencing Services."*

**Please confirm** the minimum-cover wording is appropriate.

## 5. GST-inclusive fees (recent change — please check)
Clause 5.1 now states the agreed rate is **inclusive of GST** (previously
exclusive, with GST paid on top):

> *"The agreed rate is inclusive of GST. Where the Contractor is registered for
> GST, the agreed fee is treated as GST-inclusive and the Contractor will provide
> a valid tax invoice showing the GST component."*

**Please confirm** the drafting is sound (tax treatment is separately confirmed
with the accountant).

## 6. H&S / policy acknowledgement approach
Rather than a separate signed H&S form, contractors **acknowledge and complete
online induction modules** (H&S induction, hazardous substances, security & client
property, privacy & conduct), which link to Sano's finalised H&S Plan and Chemical
Register. The agreement itself carries H&S (10), Security (11) and Confidentiality
(13) obligations.

**Please confirm** this acknowledgement approach is adequate to evidence the
worker's awareness of, and agreement to, Sano's H&S and policy requirements.

## 7. Record retention
Signed agreements are stored with the worker record; uploaded ID, right-to-work,
insurance and IR330/IR330C documents are held in **private storage** (portal-only
access); sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log.

**Please confirm** the retention + access approach is appropriate (retention period,
handling of ID / right-to-work evidence, privacy).

## 8. Structure-aware entity details + authorised signatory (recent change)
The contractor agreement now captures the **contracting structure** (sole trader /
company / partnership / trust) and, for an entity, the **authorised signatory and
their capacity** (e.g. Director, Trustee, Partner). The signed agreement + PDF show
the full legal entity identity (legal name, company number, NZBN) and, in the
signature block, who signed and in what capacity on the entity's behalf. A sole
trader still signs personally. The e-signature must match the signatory's name.

**Important:** this change is to the **captured fields and the signature block only** —
**no clause wording was added or altered.** Whether the 18 clauses need any
entity-specific wording (e.g. an authorised-signing / entity-liability clause for a
company or trust) is left to you.

For company/partnership/trust signatories, the signer must also actively tick an
**authority-to-bind declaration**: *"I confirm that I am authorised to enter into
and sign this agreement on behalf of the contracting entity."* This is mandatory
for entities (not shown to a sole trader), enforced server-side, and the exact
wording + version + timestamp are frozen onto the signed agreement and shown in the
signing record. It is a **declaration by the signatory, not a Sano-verified fact**
(a separate staff verification workflow is reserved for later).

**Please confirm:** capturing the authorised signatory + capacity + authority-to-bind
declaration and showing the entity identity on the signed agreement is appropriate,
and advise whether any structure-specific clause wording should be added.
