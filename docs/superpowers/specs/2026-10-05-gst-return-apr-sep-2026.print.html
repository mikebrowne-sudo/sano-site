4Corrections made to the records

Three categorisation errors were found and corrected before this return. They are set out here rather than presented as corrected figures without explanation.

4.1 Contractor payments had been recorded as wages

Nineteen payments to contractors were filed under wages and payroll. Employee wages fall outside the GST system, whereas a GST-registered contractor's invoice carries claimable GST; holding both in one category left the records unable to show how much GST was claimable. They are now a separate contractor-payment category, and the $1,621.96 in section 2 is claimed only against the registrations listed in section 3.

Basis of preparation
  • Figures are taken from the Sano portal as at 5 October 2026 and cover 1 April to 30 September 2026.
  • Prepared on a payments basis: sales counted when received, expenses by expense date.
  • GST is calculated as 3/23 of a GST-inclusive amount, the standard New Zealand 15% method.
  • Deleted and test records are excluded throughout.
  • No GST is claimed on employee wages, IRD remittances, owner capital, director loans, or payments to contractors without a registration on file.
  • Supporting exports provided: invoices, expenses, contractor payments, bank debits, and ASB statements for the period. Tax Action also holds read-only access to the portal's finance area.
